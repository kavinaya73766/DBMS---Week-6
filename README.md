## WEEK 6– Product Review and Rating Management System

## Project Title

Product Review and Rating Management System

## Description

The Product Review and Rating Management System is a module in an E-Commerce Order Management Database System. It is used to store customer reviews and ratings for different products.

Customers can give ratings from 1 to 5 and write feedback about products. The system helps the company understand customer satisfaction and improve product quality.

## Objectives

- To store customer reviews and product ratings.
- To maintain customer feedback.
- To calculate the average rating of each product.
- To identify highly rated products.
- To find products that need improvement.
- To analyze customer satisfaction.

## Technologies Used

- Database: MySQL
- Tool: MySQL Workbench
- Language: SQL

## Tables Used

1\. Customer Table: Stores customer details.

2\. Product Table: Stores product information.

3\. Review Table: Stores customer reviews, ratings, and review dates.

## Main Operations

1. Create the Review table.
2. Insert customer reviews and ratings.
3. Display all product reviews.
4. Display customer feedback.
5. Find the most reviewed products.
6. Display recent reviews.
7. Calculate average product ratings.
8. Generate review reports.

## Review Reports

- Product Rating Analysis
- Customer Feedback Analysis
- Rating Distribution Report
- Highly Rated Products Report
- Products Requiring Improvement Report

## Conclusion

The Product Review and Rating Management System helps the company collect and analyze customer feedback. It identifies popular products, measures customer satisfaction, and helps improve product quality based on customer reviews.
